Kaypan GeoSystems — Geodetic Engine

Engineered for professional land surveyors and civil/geodetic engineers. Runs 100% offline as a Progressive Web App with automatic local browser storage.

  • • Ellipsoids: Clarke 1880 Modified (a=6378249.14533) & WGS84
  • • Angular Input: D.MMSS (e.g. 125.3045 = 125° 30' 45")
  • • Survey Convention: Y = Easting, X = Northing
  • • Adjustments: Bowditch Rule, 2D Helmert Conformal, Tienstra
